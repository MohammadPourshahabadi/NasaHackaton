How Aboard works

On long missions astronauts must spot health changes themselves, often minutes away from Earth by radio. Aboard turns a quick daily check-in into a clear answer: how am I compared with my baseline, and what should I do next?

1 · Log it your way

Type a sentence, take a photo of a device screen, or answer the six-question wellbeing check. A local language model pulls out the readings and the body area. Every number must appear in what you wrote or on the screen, so the model cannot invent values.

2 · Compare with a baseline

Heart rate, blood pressure, serum labs and sleep are compared with NASA data: Apollo AP005, Apollo AP004 and NASA Standard Measures. Where NASA has no published range (calf ultrasound and eye OCT), each crew member gets a clearly labelled simulated preflight baseline.

3 · Act on it

Transparent rules decide green, yellow or red and list the next steps, such as repeating a measurement, a vision check for possible SANS, or contacting the crew medical officer. The AI explains the result in plain words but never changes it.

NASA data used

  • Apollo AP004 Clinical Biochemistry: serum sodium, potassium, creatinine and glucose (preflight mean ± 2 SD, n=33).
  • Apollo AP005 Cardiovascular Evaluations: resting heart rate and blood pressure (preflight supine, n=24).
  • NASA Standard Measures sleep study: in-flight sleep, 7.33 h average.
  • ISS Tele-SUS and NASA Fluid Shifts: measurement methods for calf ultrasound and eye OCT.
  • NASA Spaceflight Standard Measures: topics of the daily wellbeing questions.

Super Intelligence (SI) disclosure

Code was written with OpenAI Codex and Anthropic Claude Code from our instructions, then reviewed and tested by the team. Illustrations and animations were made with Codex and carry a visible SI-generated watermark. The demo crew (Mo, Parisa, Nayan) and their 60-day histories were generated with Claude as random values in normal human ranges, around the NASA means above. No real astronaut records are used. A local language model (Llama 3.2 3B via Ollama) reads entries, explains results and answers questions on this server; nothing is sent to an outside AI service. Full declaration: AI-DECLARATION.md.
