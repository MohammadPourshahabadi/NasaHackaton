# Aboard data sources

| Source | What Aboard uses |
| --- | --- |
| [Apollo AP004 clinical biochemistry](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19760005589) | Historical preflight serum sodium, potassium, creatinine and glucose, from the archived workbook JMAPAP004_127.xls. The optional comparison requires matching specimen and method. |
| [Apollo AP005 cardiovascular archive](https://nlsp.nasa.gov/search/data/details/e1fa5c55-ddfe-5548-91ba-864080319f44?dataType=EXPERIMENTS) and [Apollo cardiovascular report](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19760005591) | Table 6 resting heart rate, systolic and diastolic pressure after five minutes of supine rest. |
| [NASA Standard Measures sleep study](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20230003916) | In-flight wrist actigraphy summary, 2,137 nights from 19 astronauts. |
| [ISS Tele-SUS study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8047175/) | In-flight protocol and study context for panoramic ultrasound measurement of medial gastrocnemius cross-sectional area, recorded separately by leg. |
| [NASA Fluid Shifts OCT study](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20220007226) | Published in-flight TRT250 summary and measurement definition. Left-eye study results are displayed as context. Aboard records either eye separately. |
| [NASA Spaceflight Standard Measures](https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/spaceflight-standard-measures-characterizing-how-humans-adapt-in-space/) and [NASA behavioral measures research](https://taskbook.nasaprs.com/tbp/index.cfm?action=taskbook_content&uid=0537B5A7B98A78002A73E5ECA86E55B1) | Research informing the daily check-in topics. The six prompts and score bands are Aboard adaptations. |
| [96-E400 muscle study](https://doi.org/10.1152/japplphysiol.91578.2008) and [extracted MRI volume CSV](<Dataset/New for model/NASA-96-E400-calf-MRI-volume.csv>) | Historical calf MRI volume and dynamometer records from the NASA archive. Archived measurements only. |
| [LSAH preflight and postflight OCT study](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaophthalmology/fullarticle/2667551) | Published global RNFL reference used by the previous build. Archived measurements only. |

Mo, Parisa, Nayan and Admin have simulated histories. The source data and published findings were used for comparison or study context, without retraining the app's language model.
